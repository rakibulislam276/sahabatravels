Overview

A short, easy break in Rajasthan’s only hill station and its loveliest lake city: Nakki Lake, Sunset Point and the Dilwara temples at Mount Abu, then palaces and lake boat rides in Udaipur.

Mount AbuUdaipur

Tour Highlights

  • Nakki Lake boating & Sunset Point, Mount Abu
  • Dilwara Jain temples & Guru Shikhar
  • City Palace & Jagdish Temple, Udaipur
  • Lake Pichola boat ride
  • Saheliyon ki Bari & Fateh Sagar Lake

Day-wise Itinerary

  1. Day 1 Arrive Abu Road → Mount Abu

    Pick-up from Abu Road station (or Udaipur airport) and drive up to Mount Abu. Evening boating on Nakki Lake and Sunset Point.

    • Transfer to Mount Abu
    • Nakki Lake boating
    • Sunset Point
    Meals: Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Mount Abu
  2. Day 2 Mount Abu Sightseeing

    Dilwara Jain temples, Guru Shikhar (highest point of the Aravallis), Achalgarh Fort and Toad Rock.

    • Dilwara temples
    • Guru Shikhar
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Mount Abu
  3. Day 3 Mount Abu → Udaipur

    Drive about 3.5 hours to Udaipur. Evening at Fateh Sagar Lake.

    • Drive Mount Abu → Udaipur
    • Fateh Sagar Lake
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Udaipur
  4. Day 4 Udaipur Sightseeing

    City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon ki Bari and a sunset boat ride on Lake Pichola.

    • City Palace
    • Lake Pichola boat ride
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Udaipur
  5. Day 5 Depart Udaipur

    Transfer to Udaipur airport or railway station.

    • Hotel-to-airport/station transfer
    Meals: Breakfast

FAQs

How long is the Hill & Lakes Trails tour?

4 nights / 5 days — 2 nights in Mount Abu and 2 in Udaipur; it can be extended.

How do we reach Mount Abu from Mumbai?

By train to Abu Road station or by flight to Udaipur; we arrange the pick-up from either.
