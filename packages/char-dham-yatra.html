Char Dham Yatra

  • 10 Nights / 11 Days
  • Haridwar, Barkot, Uttarkashi, Guptkashi, Badrinath, Rudraprayag
  • Family
  • May to June and September to October (temples open late April/May to November)

  • Itinerary
  • Inclusions
  • Hotels
  • FAQs

Overview

The complete Char Dham Yatra of Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath from Haridwar, with the Kedarnath leg by helicopter to save the long trek.

HaridwarBarkotUttarkashiGuptkashiBadrinathRudraprayag

Tour Highlights

  • Ganga aarti at Har Ki Pauri, Haridwar
  • Yamunotri darshan (trek / pony / palki)
  • Gangotri darshan
  • Kedarnath by helicopter
  • Badrinath darshan & Mana village
  • Devprayag confluence

Day-wise Itinerary

  1. Day 1 Arrive Haridwar

    Pick-up from Dehradun airport or Haridwar station. Evening Ganga aarti at Har Ki Pauri.

    • Arrival transfer
    • Ganga aarti
    Meals: Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Haridwar
  2. Day 2 Haridwar → Barkot

    Drive via Mussoorie and Kempty Falls to Barkot.

    • Drive Haridwar → Barkot
    • Kempty Falls
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Barkot
  3. Day 3 Yamunotri Darshan

    Drive to Janki Chatti and trek (or pony/palki, extra) about 6 km to Yamunotri for darshan; return to Barkot.

    • Yamunotri trek
    • Yamunotri darshan
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Barkot
  4. Day 4 Barkot → Uttarkashi

    Drive to Uttarkashi; visit the Kashi Vishwanath temple.

    • Drive Barkot → Uttarkashi
    • Kashi Vishwanath, Uttarkashi
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Uttarkashi
  5. Day 5 Gangotri Darshan

    Day trip to Gangotri via Harsil for darshan; return to Uttarkashi.

    • Uttarkashi ⇄ Gangotri
    • Gangotri darshan
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Uttarkashi
  6. Day 6 Uttarkashi → Guptkashi

    Long scenic drive to Guptkashi along the Mandakini river.

    • Drive Uttarkashi → Guptkashi
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Guptkashi
  7. Day 7 Kedarnath by Helicopter

    Transfer to the helipad (Phata/Sersi/Guptkashi) and fly to Kedarnath for darshan; return by helicopter the same day.

    • Helicopter to Kedarnath
    • Kedarnath darshan
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Guptkashi
  8. Day 8 Guptkashi → Badrinath

    Drive to Badrinath via Joshimath. Evening darshan and Tapt Kund.

    • Drive Guptkashi → Badrinath
    • Badrinath darshan
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Badrinath
  9. Day 9 Mana Village → Rudraprayag

    Morning darshan, then Mana village (Vyas Gufa, Bhim Pul) and drive to Rudraprayag.

    • Mana village
    • Drive to Rudraprayag
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Rudraprayag
  10. Day 10 Rudraprayag → Haridwar

    Drive back via Devprayag (confluence of the Alaknanda and Bhagirathi) and Rishikesh.

    • Devprayag confluence
    • Drive to Haridwar
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Haridwar
  11. Day 11 Depart Haridwar

    Transfer to Dehradun airport or Haridwar station.

    • Departure transfer
    Meals: Breakfast

FAQs

Is the Kedarnath helicopter guaranteed?

Helicopter tickets are issued through the official IRCTC/UCADA system and depend on weather; we book as early as the window opens.

Is registration required for Char Dham?

Yes — Uttarakhand tourism registration is mandatory for every pilgrim; we complete it for you.
