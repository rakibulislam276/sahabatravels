Overview

Beaches, forts and old churches in a long weekend: North Goa’s beaches and Fort Aguada, South Goa’s churches and Colva, and a sunset cruise on the Mandovi.

North GoaSouth Goa

Tour Highlights

  • North Goa — Calangute, Baga & Anjuna beaches
  • Fort Aguada & Chapora Fort
  • Basilica of Bom Jesus, Old Goa
  • Colva & Miramar beaches, South Goa
  • Mandovi river sunset cruise

Day-wise Itinerary

  1. Day 1 Arrive Goa

    Meals: Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Goa

    • Mandovi sunset cruise

FAQs

Is Incredible Goa good for a short honeymoon?

Yes — 3 nights with both North and South Goa covered and free evenings for the beach.

Can we travel by train from Mumbai?

Yes — we arrange pick-up from Madgaon or Thivim station as well as the airport.
