Overview

Rehmat Umrah is a complete 15-day Umrah from Mumbai: 9 nights in Makkah and 5 nights in Madinah. Flights, visa, hotel accommodation, daily meals, guided Ziarat in both holy cities and every transfer are arranged, with an experienced group leader guiding the Umrah so you can focus entirely on worship.

MakkahMadinah

Tour Highlights

  • 9 nights Makkah + 5 nights Madinah
  • Guided Umrah with an experienced group leader
  • Ziarat of Jabal al-Noor, Jabal Thawr, Mina, Muzdalifah & Arafat
  • Madinah Ziarat: Masjid Quba, Uhud & Masjid al-Qiblatain
  • Rawdah appointment via Nusuk
  • Daily meals and all transfers included

Day-wise Itinerary

  1. Day 1 Mumbai → Jeddah · Perform Umrah

    Pre-departure briefing and Ihram at Mumbai airport. Fly to Jeddah, then travel by AC coach to Makkah. After check-in and rest, perform Umrah — Tawaf, Sa’i and Halq/Taqsir — together with the group leader.

    • Flight Mumbai → Jeddah (JED)
    • AC coach Jeddah → Makkah
    • Guided Umrah
    Meals: Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Makkah
  2. Day 2–7 Ibadah in Makkah

    Five daily prayers in Masjid al-Haram, nafl Tawaf and Qur’an recitation, with short talks by the group leader on the virtues and etiquette of the Haram.

    • Prayers in Masjid al-Haram
    • Group learning sessions
    Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Makkah
  3. Day 8 Makkah Ziarat

    Guided Ziarat by coach: Jabal al-Noor (Cave of Hira), Jabal Thawr, Mina, Muzdalifah, Arafat and Jabal al-Rahmah, with an optional second Umrah from Masjid Aisha (Taneem).

    • Ziarat coach tour
    • Jabal al-Noor & Jabal Thawr
    • Mina, Muzdalifah & Arafat
    Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Makkah
  4. Day 9 Ibadah · Tawaf al-Wida

    A final day of worship in the Haram, ending with the farewell Tawaf.

    • Tawaf al-Wida
    Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Makkah
  5. Day 10 Makkah → Madinah

    Travel to Madinah by AC coach (Haramain train on request) and check in near Masjid an-Nabawi.

    • Coach Makkah → Madinah
    Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Madinah
  6. Day 11–13 Masjid an-Nabawi & Rawdah

    Prayers at the Prophet’s Mosque ﷺ, Salam at the Rawdah (Nusuk appointment booked for you) and visits to Jannat al-Baqi.

    • Prayers in Masjid an-Nabawi
    • Rawdah visit via Nusuk
    Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Madinah
  7. Day 14 Madinah Ziarat

    Guided Ziarat: Masjid Quba, Mount Uhud and the martyrs’ graveyard, Masjid al-Qiblatain, the site of the Battle of the Trench and a date farm.

    • Ziarat coach tour
    • Masjid Quba & Masjid al-Qiblatain
    • Mount Uhud
    Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Madinah
  8. Day 15 Return to Mumbai

    Transfer to Madinah (or Jeddah) airport for the flight home with your Zamzam allowance.

    • Hotel-to-airport transfer
    • Flight Madinah/Jeddah → Mumbai
    Meals: Breakfast

FAQs

What documents are needed for the Umrah visa?

A passport valid for at least 6 months, a recent white-background photo and any vaccination certificate required at the time of travel. We complete the visa and Nusuk registration for you.

Can I upgrade the hotel or room sharing?

Yes. Hotel category, distance from the Haram and room sharing (quad / triple / double) can all be changed — ask us for the upgraded price.
