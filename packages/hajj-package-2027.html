Overview

A 21-day Hajj with every rite guided, from the first Umrah to Tawaf al-Wida. Stay in Makkah before Hajj, live in air-conditioned Mina tents through the days of Tashreeq, stand at Arafat with your group, then end the journey with a peaceful week in Madinah. Seats depend on the Hajj quota allotted to private tour operators — register early.

MakkahMinaArafatMuzdalifahMadinah

Tour Highlights

  • Complete guidance through all Hajj rites
  • Air-conditioned Mina tents with mattresses and catering
  • Dedicated Muallim and Indian group leader
  • Pre-departure Hajj training sessions in Mumbai
  • Madinah stay near Masjid an-Nabawi
  • Hajj kit, Ihram and guidebook

Day-wise Itinerary

  1. Day 1–2 Mumbai → Makkah · Umrah

    Pre-departure briefing and Ihram (Tamattu’) in Mumbai. Fly to Jeddah, transfer to Makkah and perform Umrah with the group. Rest and settle in.

    • Flight Mumbai → Jeddah
    • AC coach to Makkah
    • Umrah al-Tamattu’
    Meals: Full board Overnight: Hotel, Makkah (Aziziyah or Haram area)
  2. Day 3–7 Preparation in Makkah

    Prayers in Masjid al-Haram, daily Hajj workshops on the rites of each day, and Ziyarat of the holy sites so you know the ground before Hajj begins.

    • Daily Hajj workshops
    • Makkah Ziyarat
    Meals: Full board Overnight: Hotel, Makkah
  3. Day 8 8 Dhul Hijjah · Yawm al-Tarwiyah · Mina

    Enter Ihram for Hajj and move to the Mina tents. Pray Dhuhr to Fajr in Mina, following the Sunnah.

    • AC tents in Mina
    Meals: Full board Overnight: Mina tents
  4. Day 9 9 Dhul Hijjah · Day of Arafat · Muzdalifah

    Move to Arafat after sunrise for Wuquf — the heart of Hajj — with du’a until sunset. Proceed to Muzdalifah, pray Maghrib and Isha combined, collect pebbles and spend the night under the open sky.

    • Wuquf at Arafat
    • Night at Muzdalifah
    Meals: Full board Overnight: Arafat camp / Muzdalifah
  5. Day 10 Eid al-Adha · Rami · Qurbani · Tawaf al-Ifadah

    Return to Mina for Rami of Jamarat al-Aqabah, Qurbani (arranged through the official coupon system), Halq, then Tawaf al-Ifadah and Sa’i in Makkah.

    • Rami at Jamarat
    • Tawaf al-Ifadah & Sa’i
    Meals: Full board Overnight: Mina tents
  6. Day 11–13 Days of Tashreeq in Mina

    Stay in Mina and stone all three Jamarat each day, with the group leader timing each Rami for safety. Return to Makkah after Rami on the 12th or 13th.

    • Daily Rami of the three Jamarat
    • Mina tents
    Meals: Full board Overnight: Mina tents / Makkah hotel
  7. Day 14–15 Makkah · Tawaf al-Wida

    Rest, prayer in the Haram, and the farewell Tawaf before leaving Makkah.

    • Tawaf al-Wida
    Meals: Full board Overnight: Hotel, Makkah
  8. Day 16–20 Madinah

    Travel to Madinah and stay near Masjid an-Nabawi. Salam at the Rawdah, prayers in the Prophet’s Mosque ﷺ and Ziyarat of Quba, Uhud and al-Qiblatain.

    • Coach / train Makkah → Madinah
    • Masjid an-Nabawi & Rawdah
    • Madinah Ziyarat
    Meals: Full board Overnight: Hotel, Madinah
  9. Day 21 Return to Mumbai

    Transfer to Madinah airport for the flight home with Zamzam as per airline allowance.

    • Flight Madinah → Mumbai
    Meals: Breakfast

FAQs

When should I register for Hajj 2027?

As early as possible. Private Hajj seats depend on the quota allotted by the Ministry of Minority Affairs, and registration usually opens several months before Hajj.

What is included in the Hajj package price?

Flights, Hajj visa, accommodation in Makkah, Mina, Arafat and Madinah, full-board meals, transfers and guidance. Qurbani is paid at actual cost.
