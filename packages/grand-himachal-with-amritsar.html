Grand Himachal with Amritsar

  • 11 Nights / 12 Days
  • Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Dalhousie, Amritsar

Overview

Himachal end to end, finishing in Amritsar. Colonial Shimla, Manali and the Solang Valley snow, the Tibetan quarter of McLeod Ganj in Dharamshala, the meadows of Dalhousie and Khajjiar, and finally the Golden Temple and the Wagah Border ceremony.

ShimlaManaliDharamshalaDalhousieAmritsar

Tour Highlights

  • Mall Road, Kufri & Jakhoo, Shimla
  • Solang Valley & Atal Tunnel / Rohtang (permit), Manali
  • McLeod Ganj & Bhagsu waterfall, Dharamshala
  • Khajjiar — ‘mini Switzerland of India’, Dalhousie
  • Golden Temple, Jallianwala Bagh & Wagah Border, Amritsar

Day-wise Itinerary

  1. Day 1 Arrive Chandigarh → Shimla

    Pick-up from Chandigarh airport/railway station and drive about 4 hours to Shimla. Evening on Mall Road and the Ridge.

    • Drive Chandigarh → Shimla
    • Mall Road & the Ridge
    Meals: Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Shimla
  2. Day 2 Kufri & Shimla Sightseeing

    Excursion to Kufri, then Jakhoo Temple, Christ Church and the Viceregal Lodge (outside).

    • Kufri excursion
    • Shimla heritage sights
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Shimla
  3. Day 3 Shimla → Manali

    Drive to Manali along the Beas river, via Pandoh Dam and the Kullu valley (river rafting optional at Kullu).

    • Drive Shimla → Manali
    • Optional rafting at Kullu
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Manali
  4. Day 4 Solang Valley & Atal Tunnel

    Day trip to Solang Valley for paragliding, ropeway and snow activities, and through the Atal Tunnel to Sissu (Rohtang Pass on permit).

    • Solang Valley
    • Atal Tunnel & Sissu
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Manali
  5. Day 5 Manali Local Sightseeing

    Hadimba Temple, Vashisht hot springs, Manu Temple, Tibetan monastery and Old Manali.

    • Hadimba Temple
    • Vashisht hot springs
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Manali
  6. Day 6 Manali → Dharamshala

    Drive to Dharamshala via Baijnath Temple.

    • Drive Manali → Dharamshala
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Dharamshala
  7. Day 7 Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj

    Dalai Lama Temple complex, Bhagsu Nag waterfall, St John in the Wilderness church and the HPCA cricket stadium.

    • Dalai Lama Temple
    • Bhagsu waterfall
    • HPCA stadium
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Dharamshala
  8. Day 8 Dharamshala → Dalhousie

    Drive to Dalhousie. Evening walk at Gandhi Chowk and Subhash Chowk.

    • Drive Dharamshala → Dalhousie
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Dalhousie
  9. Day 9 Khajjiar Excursion

    Day trip to Khajjiar’s meadow and lake, ringed by deodar forest, with an optional visit to Chamba.

    • Khajjiar meadow & lake
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Dalhousie
  10. Day 10 Dalhousie → Amritsar · Wagah Border

    Drive to Amritsar and continue to the Attari–Wagah Border for the Beating Retreat ceremony.

    • Drive Dalhousie → Amritsar
    • Wagah Border ceremony
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Amritsar
  11. Day 11 Golden Temple & Amritsar

    Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib), Jallianwala Bagh, Partition Museum and the old-city bazaars.

    • Golden Temple
    • Jallianwala Bagh
    Meals: Breakfast, Dinner Overnight: Hotel, Amritsar
  12. Day 12 Depart Amritsar

    Transfer to Amritsar airport or railway station.

    • Hotel-to-airport/station transfer
    Meals: Breakfast

FAQs

Where does the Grand Himachal tour start and end?

It starts at Chandigarh and ends at Amritsar, both with good flight and train connections to Mumbai.

Will we see snow?

Snow is likely at Solang/Atal Tunnel from December to April; at Rohtang until about June.
