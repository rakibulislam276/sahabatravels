What you’ll usually need

Exact requirements depend on the country and visa type — your checklist will be specific to you.

  • Passport

    Valid 6+ months from return, 2 blank pages, old passports if any

  • Photographs

    Recent photos to the embassy’s size and background rules

  • Financials

    Bank statements (3–6 months), ITRs or salary slips

  • Cover letter & forms

    Purpose of travel, itinerary and signed application form

  • Bookings

    Return flight reservation and hotel booking

FAQs

Visa questions, answered

Destination list last reviewed: October 2026. Visa rules for Indian passport holders change often; we confirm the current rule for your trip.

Which visas does Sahaba Travels help with?

Tourist, business, Umrah, family-visit, student and transit visas for 45+ destinations, including Dubai, Saudi Arabia, Schengen, the UK, the USA, Canada, Australia and Asia.

What documents are usually needed?

A passport valid for at least 6 months with two blank pages, recent photos, bank statements for the last 3–6 months, income tax returns or salary slips, a cover letter, flight and hotel bookings, and travel insurance where required. We send a destination-specific checklist.

How long does a visa take?

e-visas usually take 1–7 working days, Schengen and UK visas about 15 working days, and US visas depend on interview availability. Apply at least 3–4 weeks before travel; earlier in peak season.

Can Sahaba guarantee a visa?

No agent can guarantee a visa; the decision is the embassy’s. We make sure your file is complete and correct, which gives you the best chance of approval.

Are visa fees refundable if the visa is refused?

Embassy fees and service charges are non-refundable once an application is submitted, whatever the outcome. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

Do you offer doorstep document pickup in Mumbai?

Yes. We can collect documents and deliver passports across Mumbai on request.
