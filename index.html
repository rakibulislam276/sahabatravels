FAQs

Questions, Answered

Quick answers about our services, documents and forex. Still unsure? WhatsApp us — we reply fast.

Where is Sahaba Travels located?

Shop No. 2, Arsiwala Building, Dimtimkar Road, Nagpada, Mumbai – 400008. We are open Monday to Friday 10 AM–9 PM and Saturday 10 AM–6 PM; closed on Sunday.

What services does Sahaba Travels offer?

Umrah and Hajj packages, international and domestic holiday packages, visa assistance, flight and hotel bookings, and foreign exchange including currency notes and forex travel cards.

What documents are needed for an Umrah visa from India?

A passport valid for at least 6 months, a recent passport-size photo with a white background, and vaccination certificates where required. Our team handles the visa application and Nusuk registration.

What do I need to buy foreign currency in Mumbai?

As per RBI rules you need your passport, a valid visa, a confirmed air ticket and your PAN card. Cash is limited to the equivalent of USD 3,000 per trip; the balance can be loaded on a forex card.

How are your forex rates calculated?

The buy rate on our website follows the live interbank market and refreshes automatically; our sell rate is updated daily by our forex desk. Call us to lock the final rate before you transact.

Can I customise a holiday or Umrah package?

Yes. Dates, hotels, room sharing, number of nights and sightseeing can all be changed. Use Plan Your Trip or WhatsApp us on +91 97690 47633.
